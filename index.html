Profile

Building bridges between data and discovery.

My work combines statistical modelling, large-scale biological data analysis, molecular simulation, and modern machine learning.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at AstraZeneca’s Centre for Genomics Research, where I develop computational methods for genomic and multi-omic data analysis.

My research spans population genetics, phenome-wide association studies, whole-genome sequencing, functional annotation, and AI-assisted integration of genomic, phenotypic, and clinical evidence.

Previously, I completed a PhD in Applied Mathematics at EPFL, focusing on sequence-dependent mechanics of double-stranded nucleic acids.

Research

Current research themes

Methods and biological questions that define my current research.

01 / GENOMICS

Human germline mutational signatures

Characterising single-nucleotide, double-nucleotide, and insertion-deletion mutational signatures across hundreds of thousands of whole genomes and diverse ancestries.

02 / NON-CODING DNA

G-quadruplex variation and disease susceptibility

Identifying disease-associated and functional G-quadruplexes through mechanistic aggregate models and integration of multi-omic evidence.

03 / CLONAL HAEMATOPOIESIS

Ageing, mutational processes, and sequencing artefacts

Quantifying age-related and sequencing-related mutational signatures in clonal haematopoiesis and control samples.

04 / AI FOR SCIENCE

AI-assisted evidence integration

Building workflows that use LLMs and agentic AI to explore, integrate, and summarise genomic, phenotypic, clinical, and functional evidence.

05 / DNA MECHANICS

Sequence-dependent mechanics of nucleic acids

Developing coarse-grained and physics-informed models to understand how DNA sequence influences shape, flexibility, and interactions.

06 / BIOMECHANICS

Machine learning for motion capture

Creating real-time models that translate portable motion-capture data into clinically and biomechanically meaningful outputs.
